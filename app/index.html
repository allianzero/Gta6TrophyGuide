Roadmap to the Platinum

    Before you start — read this first

    • Cheats off. PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies — and GameSpot and PC Gamer add that you cannot save any more once you have used one.
    • No mission replay. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission; those saves are your only way back.
    • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot as you find it, starting with Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1.
    • No online trophies. The whole list is single-player, so the Platinum needs no online play at all.
